\section*{Forty-metric summary}
For the selected CES-FL R2 reference, 14 of 40 point estimates are numerically favorable to Ding under the preferred direction. This is descriptive only: fairness thresholds and test inventories are not confirmed to match. R2 AUC is above Ding's reported AUC on FF++ (98.40 vs 97.71), and DFD (82.12 vs 81.46), and below on DFDC (59.52 vs 61.86) and Celeb-DF (74.67 vs 76.75). No multi-seed uncertainty estimates are available.
